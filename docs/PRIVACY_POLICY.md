## Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how **Adventures Me** (operated by Superjet 650 / Green-N-Cleen Services LLC, referred to as "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, processes, stores, and protects your personal data and geolocation records when you use the **Adventures Me** mobile application (the "App") and our website located at [https://adventuresme.com](https://adventuresme.com) (the "Site", collectively the "Services").

By accessing or using our Services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our Services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, information collected automatically during your use of the App, and information generated by your interactions with our Services.

### A. Personal Information You Provide
- **Account Registration:** When you register an account, we collect your name, username (`@handle`), email address, password, phone number, country, gender, and date of birth.
- **Profile Information:** Optional details you add to your profile, including your profile avatar/photo and "About Me" bio.
- **Support & Inquiries:** Any information you provide when contacting customer support or communicating with us.

### B. Geolocation & Travel Tracking Data
To power our automated travel tracking, checkpoint creation, and interactive map features, we collect location data from your device:
- **Foreground Location:** GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, altitude), heading, and timestamps recorded while the App is open and active.
- **Background Location:** When you enable the **Travel Tracker** mode for a trip, we collect location data in the background (even when the App is closed or not in use) to record travel routes, calculate distances, recognize checkpoints, and log visited countries.
- **Precision Preferences:** You may configure tracking accuracy settings (such as toggling between High Accuracy and Battery Saver modes) which adjust the frequency and granularity of GPS data captured.

### C. User-Generated Content & Media
- **Photographs:** Photos captured via your camera or selected from your photo library attached to specific trips, itinerary days, or profile avatars.
- **Audio Recordings & Voice Notes:** Audio clips recorded using your device’s microphone to create voice memories associated with trip checkpoints.
- **Trip Metadata & Notes:** Trip titles, destination names, itinerary notes, custom checkpoint tags, dates, and journal entries.

### D. Automatically Generated Content (AI Stories)
- **AI Story Generation:** When you request an AI story for your trip, our automated processing models analyze your trip metadata (dates, checkpoints, notes, and selected photos) to generate personalized travel narratives, story summaries, and daily highlight reels.

### E. Usage, Device & Log Data
- **Device Information:** Device model, operating system version (iOS/Android), unique device identifiers, language settings, and timezone.
- **App Analytics & Diagnostics:** Crash logs, network performance data, error reports, and feature engagement metrics (such as trip view counts).
- **Local Storage:** Secure locally cached preferences, tokens, and offline checkpoint data stored on your device using encrypted storage.

To provide core travel features, the App requests permission to access certain hardware and system capabilities:

| Permission | Platform | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Location (When In Use)** | iOS & Android | Displays your current position on the map, finds nearby places, and tags checkpoints. |
| **Location (Always / Background)** | iOS & Android | Powers automated background route recording and checkpoint logging when "Travel Tracker" is active. |
| **Camera** | iOS & Android | Allows you to take photos directly inside the App to add as trip memories. |

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:
- **Core Functionality:** Create and maintain your account, record your journeys, plot interactive route maps, organize itinerary checkpoints, and display trip memories.
- **AI Processing:** Analyze your journey data and media to automatically craft readable travel stories, highlights, and summaries.
- **Social & Sharing Features:** Enable public trip feeds, shareable links, and QR codes when you choose to share a trip publicly or with specific friends.
- **App Optimization:** Troubleshoot technical issues, analyze usage trends, and improve battery efficiency during active tracking.
- **Safety & Security:** Detect and prevent fraudulent activities, unauthorized access, and security incidents.
- **Communications:** Send account verification codes, essential service updates, and responses to support inquiries.

## 4. How Your Information Is Shared

We respect your privacy and **never sell your personal data** to third parties. We share data only under the following circumstances:

### A. User-Controlled Sharing & Public Trips
- **Private Trips (Default):** Your trips, itineraries, checkpoints, photos, and voice notes are private to your account by default.
- **Public & Shared Trips:** If you toggle a trip to "Public" or share a trip via a direct link or QR code token, other users and visitors can view the trip title, author name, route checkpoints, photos, and generated stories. You can change trip visibility back to private at any time.

### B. Third-Party Service Providers
We partner with trusted service providers to support our infrastructure:
- **Mapping & Geocoding:** Google Maps SDK / Google Cloud Platform (for map rendering, address resolution, and route calculation).
- **Cloud Infrastructure & Hosting:** Secure cloud servers and database providers that store encrypted user data, media files, and backups.
- **AI Service Providers:** Secure cloud APIs used to process text and metadata to produce AI trip stories. Data sent for processing is not used to train public third-party models.

### C. Legal Compliance & Protection
We may disclose information if required to do so by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, and safety of Adventures Me, our users, or the public.

## 6. Your Rights and Account Deletion (Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v) Compliant)

We provide full transparency and control over your personal data:

### In-App Account Deletion
You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time directly within the mobile application:
1. Open the **Adventures Me** App.
2. Go to **Profile** > **Edit Profile**.
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete Account**.
4. Confirm your selection.

Upon confirming deletion:
- Your user profile, personal information, authentication credentials, and account records will be permanently deleted from our primary databases.
- All associated trips, checkpoints, location tracking logs, uploaded photographs, voice memories, and AI-generated stories will be permanently erased.
- Any publicly shared links or QR codes associated with your account will immediately become invalid.

### Additional Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)
Depending on your location, you may have additional statutory rights:
- **Right to Access / Portability:** Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Right to Rectification:** Update or correct inaccurate profile information directly in the App.
- **Right to Restrict or Object:** Withdraw consent for background location tracking at any time via device settings.
